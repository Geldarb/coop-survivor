# Pony Pals: Rainbow Rescue — How to play

A cute 2-player co-op game (and solo) for kids and grown-up pals. You only move; your powers fly by themselves. Pop the grumpy baddies, collect sparkles, and open presents.

Play online: **https://geldarb.github.io/coop-survivor/** (or open `index.html`).

## Start
1. Type your name.
2. **Play** starts a solo game.
3. **Play with a friend**: one pal hosts and sends the 4-letter code; the other types it and taps **Join**. Then the host taps **Start!**
4. **Ponies** — pick a pony and your colours. **Pony Shop** — spend Hearts on treats that help every play. **Sticker Book** — pictures of what you have found.

Tip: add `?join=CODE` to the web address to pre-fill a friend's code.

## Move
- **W A S D** or **arrow keys**, or drag on the screen.
- Level up: tap a card, or press **1 / 2 / 3 / 4**.
- New cards: **R** (if you bought New Cards in the shop).
- Open a present: **Enter**, **Space** or **E**.
- Solo pause: the **Pause** button, or **Esc** / **P**. The meadow also pauses if you switch tabs.

## The idea
Grumps go **poof** into sparkles and hearts. You collect **Hearts** during a play and at the end. If your pal gets sleepy, nuzzle them (stand close) to wake them up. The play ends when both pals are tuckered out.

A present from a big grump can **super-up** a power: take that power to level 6, hold its special bit, then open the present. Recipes are in the Sticker Book and as a tiny picture on the level-up cards.

## Ponies and colours
There are 13 ponies in different colours (Poppy Pink is the first free one). In **Ponies**, pick a **body colour** and a **mane colour** (including a rainbow mane). Your choice is saved on this device and your friend will see it in co-op.

Some ponies unlock when you do something special (or you can buy them with Hearts).

## Solo pause
The pause menu shows your time, poofs, level, Hearts so far, and your powers. **Start over** and **Go home** first ask, then add the Hearts you have earned — just like a normal ending.

Co-op games do not pause, so keep an eye on your pal.

*Pony Pals and Rainbow Meadow are original. This is not My Little Pony or any other existing pony world.*
